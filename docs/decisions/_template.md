# NNNN — Short title in the imperative

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by 00NN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision. Facts and measurements, not opinions. If a number is quoted here, say
where it was measured and with which tool.

## Decision

What we are doing, in one or two sentences, in the active voice.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. An alternative that was never seriously considered does
not belong here — a list of strawmen makes the record worthless.

## Consequences

What this costs. Include the bad parts: a record that only lists benefits is marketing. Say what
becomes harder, what is now locked in, and what would have to happen to revisit it.
